Boardory: Find and Compare Boarding Houses Near CSU

What Is Boardory?

Boardory is a boarding-house information and comparison website for Ampayon, Butuan City and areas near Caraga State University Main Campus. It helps students, workers, teachers, and other renters view important accommodation details in one organized place.

Finding a place to stay can be difficult when rental details are spread across many separate social media posts. Boardory gathers key details such as monthly rent, deposits, utilities, house rules, and location so visitors can compare options before contacting an owner.

What a Boardory Listing Shows

Each boarding-house entry is designed to answer the common questions renters ask before visiting a property. Depending on what an owner shares, a listing organizes practical details clearly.

Details Included in a Listing

  • Boarding-house name and property location or address
  • Monthly rental price, security deposit, and advance payment rules
  • Water, electricity, and other utility arrangements
  • Available rooms or spaces
  • House rules, curfew, and gender policy
  • Property photos, optional room walkthroughs, and owner contact details

How to Find a Boarding House

Visitors can use Boardory in a few simple steps when looking for accommodation near Caraga State University. These steps outline how the website helps organize a boarding-house search.

  1. Review the available boarding-house information on the website.
  2. Check the monthly rent, deposit, and utility arrangements for each property.
  3. Compare house rules, facilities, and distance from campus.
  4. Open the map or original reference link to check the property location.
  5. Contact the boarding-house owner directly to confirm room availability.
