About Boardory

Why We Made Boardory

Students who live far from Caraga State University often visit several boarding houses just to ask about room rates, utilities, and house rules. Checking properties one by one takes extra time, effort, and transportation money.

Boardory was created as an academic HTML project to organize boarding-house information in one clear website. It aims to help renters compare properties more easily while giving owners a structured way to present their accommodations.

Who Boardory Is For

Boardory is designed for people looking for longer-term accommodation in Ampayon, Butuan City and nearby areas, as well as property owners in the community.

  • Caraga State University students and incoming college freshmen
  • Working individuals looking for accommodation near Ampayon
  • Teachers and university personnel
  • Boarding-house owners who want to share clear information about their properties

Our Team

We are first-year Bachelor of Science in Information Technology (BSIT) students at Caraga State University developing Boardory for ITE 10 (Introduction to Computing).
